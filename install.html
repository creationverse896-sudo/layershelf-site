Install and activate

Five minutes from your receipt email to your first scan.

1. Find your purchase email

Straight after checkout, Lemon Squeezy emails you a receipt with two things in it:

  • a download link for the LayerShelf installer, and
  • your licence key. Keep this email; you need the key on each PC.

Nothing after a few minutes? Check your spam folder for an email from Lemon Squeezy, then email us with the address you used at checkout.

2. Run the installer

Open the downloaded LayerShelf-Setup file and follow the steps. Windows will ask for permission to install, because LayerShelf installs for everyone on the PC.

Seeing “Windows protected your PC”? LayerShelf isn't code-signed yet, so Windows shows this blue screen for new apps. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

If your PC has Smart App Control switched on, Windows blocks unsigned apps completely and offers no Run anyway button. Email us before changing any security settings: we'll help, or refund you if it can't be sorted.

3. Activate LayerShelf

  1. Open LayerShelf from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut. The first time, a short setup screen appears: choose Skip setup for now.
  2. In the left-hand menu, choose Licence & sync.
  3. Paste your licence key under Activate this PC and choose Activate.

Until it's activated, LayerShelf opens read-only: you can look around, but not change the library. Activation needs an internet connection. After that LayerShelf works offline for 30 days at a time. When the 30 days are up it asks you to check the licence: go online, open Licence & sync and choose Check now.

4. Add your model folders

Once activated, choose Sources in the left-hand menu and add the folders to scan. Scanning only reads your files and never moves or uploads them. The first scan of a big collection can take a while; later scans only look at what changed.

Using a second PC, or moving to a new one

One licence covers 2 PCs at a time. Install LayerShelf on the other PC and activate it with the same key. To move to a new PC, open Licence & sync on the old one and choose Deactivate this PC first. If the old PC is gone, email us and we'll free up the activation.

To take your catalogue with you, use Export library on the old PC and import the file on the new one.

Uninstalling

Uninstall LayerShelf from Settings → Apps. Your model files are never touched. Your catalogue stays in %APPDATA%\cardiff-model-library-desktop, so reinstalling picks up where you left off. Deactivate the PC first if you're not coming back, then delete that folder to remove everything.
