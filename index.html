Every maker's collection, perfectly organised

LayerShelf turns the STL, 3MF and OBJ files scattered across your PC into one searchable library — and shows you which of them are the same model saved three times.

What it does

  • Finds duplicates properly. Exact copies are matched by content, so a renamed file is still caught. Same-geometry variants are matched by the shape itself, regardless of how the model was rotated, positioned or scaled.
  • One decision per group. Review duplicates side by side and keep them, group them as a project, or recycle the spares.
  • Catalogues in place. Files are never moved or altered unless you plan and confirm a move.
  • Everything else you need. Thumbnails, tags, ratings, notes, categories, materials, projects, collections, a print queue and printer notes.

Local by design

The catalogue is a database on your own PC. There is no account, no telemetry and no analytics, and this release makes no network connections at all. Your models are never uploaded. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is stored and where.

Requirements

Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit. See support for supported formats and current limitations.
